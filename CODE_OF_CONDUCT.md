# Code of conduct

SciConnect is built for people who disagree about evidence for a living. Disagree with claims, not with people.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below doesn't replace it.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to people of every background, career stage and field.
- Critique ideas, methods and code with specific, sourced reasons.
- Accept correction gracefully, and credit others' work.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming you've understood.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately misrepresenting someone's work or findings.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@elijahthinks](https://github.com/elijahthinks), through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will review every report and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
